Chef's Special

Luxury Beef Wellington

A restaurant-style Beef Wellington with tender beef fillet, mushroom duxelles and golden, buttery puff pastry.

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef tenderloin
  • 250 g mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 1 sheet puff pastry
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, black pepper and fresh thyme

How to Make It

  1. Season the beef generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear it in hot oil until browned on all sides. Brush with Dijon mustard and let it cool.
  3. Cook the chopped mushrooms with butter and thyme until their moisture evaporates completely.
  4. Spread the mushroom mixture over the pastry, place the beef in the centre and wrap tightly.
  5. Brush the pastry with beaten egg and score the top lightly for a beautiful finish.
  6. Bake at 200°C for about 25–30 minutes, until the pastry is deeply golden.
  7. Rest for 10 minutes, slice carefully and serve with your favourite sauce.
